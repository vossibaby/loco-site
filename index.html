The point of Loco

Less editing. More intention.

A small, predictable workflow for musicians who already know what they want to sound and look like.

01 / LOCAL

Your files stay yours

Rendering happens on the iPhone. Loco does not require cloud rendering or a Loco account.

02 / PRECISE

One beat, one timeline

The selected beat defines the duration. Visual framing is adjustable without changing your source artwork.

03 / RECOVERABLE

Resume with confidence

Projects, renders, uploads, and post-upload actions are tracked separately so a retry does not silently start over.

The workflow

From blank canvas to channel.

01

Choose a format

Start a Video or a 30, 45, or 55-second Shorts clip, then select 1080p or 4K.

02

Bring your media

Select one visual and one WAV or MP3 beat. Loco shows the source facts before you render.

03

Frame the visual

Keep it aspect-fit or make a deliberate zoom and pan. Choose black, color, or a blurred background.

04

Render locally

Loco creates a YouTube-ready MP4 with H.264 video, AAC-LC audio, and SDR Rec.709 output.

05

Review and upload

Review your title, description, tags, audience, thumbnail, and playlist before an explicit upload action.

Publishing with care

Transparent about YouTube.

Loco uses the official YouTube Data API and acts only on the channel the signed-in user chooses.

Private by default in development

Our current unaudited API project uploads videos as Private. Public or Unlisted controls will be enabled only after the required YouTube compliance audit and an app update.

No hidden channel access

Google authorization is requested only for YouTube actions. You can disconnect access, remove local project data, and request deletion of data held by Loco.
