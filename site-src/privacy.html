Privacy Policy

Loco is designed around local-first media preparation. This policy explains what the app stores, when it communicates with Google and YouTube, and how you can remove data.

Effective date: September 14, 2026 · Last updated: September 14, 2026

Loco is an independent application. It is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Google or YouTube.

1. Who operates Loco

Loco is an independent developer project maintained by the owner of the Loco GitHub repository. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us through the Loco support issues. Please do not post passwords, OAuth tokens, or private media in a public issue.

2. Data Loco handles

Local project data

Loco stores project settings, imported media copies, rendered videos, YouTube metadata drafts, upload progress, and recovery records on the device so projects can be resumed. This data is not sent to a Loco server.

Google and YouTube data

When you choose a YouTube action, Loco uses Google OAuth and the official YouTube Data API to identify your chosen channel, upload a video, read or write the API-supported metadata you request, upload a selected thumbnail, place a video in a selected playlist, and observe processing status. Loco does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or access channels that you did not authorize.

Credentials

OAuth credentials are handled by the Google Sign-In/iOS authorization flow and protected device storage. Loco does not ask you to type a Google password into the app and does not embed a client secret.

3. Why data is used

Data is used only to provide the workflow you start: prepare media, preserve an editable project, upload to your own channel, complete requested post-upload actions, and recover interrupted work.

4. Sharing and service providers

When you upload, the rendered video, metadata, and any selected thumbnail are transmitted directly to YouTube under your authorization. Apple system services may handle file selection, playback, storage, and background execution. Loco does not operate an analytics, advertising, or social-tracking SDK.

5. Retention and deletion

Local project data remains until you delete the project or remove the app, subject to the retention setting shown in Loco. YouTube data retained in the app is refreshed or removed according to YouTube API policies. You can use the Delete Data instructions to remove local data, disconnect Google access, and request deletion of data associated with Loco.

6. Your choices

  • Continue locally without connecting Google.
  • Cancel an upload or retry it from the retained rendered video.
  • Disconnect Google/YouTube access from the account area.
  • Delete a project and its app-owned files.
  • Change video visibility in YouTube Studio, including for videos uploaded while the API project is unaudited.

7. Children

Loco is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will identify the current version.

9. Contact

For privacy requests, use the GitHub issue tracker and title the issue “Privacy request”. We will use the details you provide only to respond to that request.
